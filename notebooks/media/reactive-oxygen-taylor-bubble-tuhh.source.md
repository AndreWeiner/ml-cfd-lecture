The website describes a reactive oxygen Taylor bubble in a copper-complex
solution in acetonitrile. Its caption and the supplied filename give different
concentrations, so the lecture deliberately does not specify a concentration.
The confined Taylor bubble is distinguished from the unconfined regime-map case.

## Horizontal display derivative

`reactive-oxygen-taylor-bubble-tuhh-horizontal.gif` rotates the original 90°
clockwise (555 × 135 pixels; bubble head on the right, wake on the left). Reproduce with
`python3 scripts/rotate_taylor_bubble_animation.py`. All 212 decoded frames,
frame durations, and loop count are checked against the rotated original.
The 10 px rounded corners and shadow are presentation styles in the page, not changes to source data.
