# Sharaf et al. (2017), experimental Figure 4 digitization

Source: D. M. Sharaf et al., *Shapes and paths of an air bubble rising in
quiescent liquids*, Physics of Fluids 29, 122104 (2017),
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5006726.
Author manuscript: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.08757 (PDF page 6).

## Scope

`points.csv` contains 183 individually discernible markers selected from the
experimental Figure 4, not the six examples previously transcribed from captions.
This is a figure digitization, not the authors' original raw dataset. The paper
reports about 300 bubbles and at least three runs per parameter combination;
Figure 4 cannot reveal individual repeat runs, exact underlying inputs, or fully
occluded markers. No missing observations or repetitions were invented.
The selection aims to cover the full visible map; completeness of hidden or
merged symbols cannot be established from the image.

Counts: 23 axisymmetric (3 spherical, 9 oblate, 11 dimpled), 40 skirted,
36 oscillatory, 84 breakup (47 brown filled squares, 37 magenta open squares).
The dense brown-square clusters required approximate centers inferred from
exposed edges; their per-point notes flag that ambiguity. These coordinates
should not be treated as exact experimental settings or as three-significant-
figure measurements despite CSV numerical formatting.

## Definitions and labels

The source uses radius-based Ga and Eo. The equivalent bubble radius is the
length scale; source values are retained without transformation beyond axis
calibration. `source_regime` retains I, II, III, IV from Sharaf. The figure's
brown squares indicate skirted breakup; magenta squares collectively indicate
satellite and toroidal breakup. Individual magenta-marker breakup subtypes are
not recoverable from their identical symbols. They are deliberately not assigned
to the lecture's separate Tripathi IV/V classes. Density/viscosity ratios and
initial bubble shapes differ from Tripathi; pooling is a separate modeling choice.

## Reproduction and verification

- `figure4-source.jpg`: unchanged embedded 2791 x 1970 JPEG extracted using
  `pdfimages -f 6 -l 6 -j /tmp/sharaf2017.pdf /tmp/sharaf`.
- `marker_centers.json`: editable, visually reviewed pixel-center selections.
- `points.csv`: logarithmic-axis calibrated coordinates and source labels.
- `extraction-overlay.png`: numbered centers on the source image for visual review.
- `audit.json`: source-image SHA256, calibration, fits, counts and limitations.

Run `.venv-quarto/bin/python scripts/extract_sharaf_regime_points.py --output /tmp/sharaf-extract`
with an initially empty output directory. The Ga ticks at 10 and 100 and the Eo
ticks at 0.1, 1, 10, 100 calibrate linear relations in log10 coordinates.
For upward triangles the geometric centroid is used, not the bounding-box center.
Manual crowded-center uncertainty is several pixels and can be larger where
outlines overlap. No formal measurement uncertainty is available from the figure.
The original PDF text is used for interpretation, with no automatic label mapping
from coordinates or assumed boundaries.

The comparison plot uses shared log-axis limits and the course C0-C9 palette,
with LaTeX-rendered text at 180 mm width. Sharaf IV remains an aggregate breakup
class in that comparison. Light and dark SVG/PNG exports are generated by
`scripts/compare_bubble_sources.py`. Both exported PNG themes and the source
overlay were visually inspected. These figures are standalone review artifacts;
they have not been inserted into the exercise or used to retrain its classifier.
